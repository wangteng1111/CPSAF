## Comparison status

Published Zhu/Choi/Herrmann static metrics and current CPSAF dynamic metrics are intentionally kept as separate benchmark families. A direct paper-quality comparison requires:

1. Current MLP evaluated on the official Herrmann 49-start static test set.
2. Zhu/Choi policies inserted into the same CPSAF continuous-time plant/timing harness.

Until these bridge tests are run, claims that CPSAF numerically outperforms Zhu on the published benchmark are not justified.
